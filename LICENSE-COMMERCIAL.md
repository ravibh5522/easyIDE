## What a commercial license adds

A commercial license replaces the noncommercial restriction with a paid grant for
your organization. Terms (seat count or entity-wide, term length, support and
update entitlements, warranty and indemnity, redistribution rights) are agreed
per customer and are set out in the signed agreement, which supersedes the
[LICENSE](LICENSE) file for the licensed party.

Buying a commercial license does not change the license of the third-party
components listed in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). Their terms -- including the
copyleft ones -- continue to apply to you independently.
