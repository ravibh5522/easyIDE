- Wrote [decision 0018](../decision/0018-editor-engine-and-decorations.md), ux-overhaul ADR-B. - Added `workspace/decor/`:
  - `DecorationModel` / `DecorationSet`: pure-Kotlin layers in UTF-16 offsets. The layers are diagnostics, document highlights, search matches, inlay hints, gutter markers and code lenses. Each layer has a priority, a drop-or-clip edit policy and per-source entries. Every write names the text its offsets refer to. `OffsetEdit` derives the edit from the prefix/suffix diff and never splits a surrogate pair.
  - Draw-phase painters: squiggle waves (hints are three dots), background ranges, end-of-line inlay ghost text and one gutter glyph per line. Painting is limited to the live line window, and the text sits in its own graphics layer, so a decoration change triggers no recomposition and no relayout.
  - `EditorGeometry` maps offsets to viewport rectangles.
  - `EditorPopup` stays inside the viewport. Escape and Back dismiss it, and it leaves focus in the text field.
- New `DecorationColors` tokens under `EditorColors.decorations`, taken from VS Code's registered defaults (checked in its source today). A `DecorationRegistry` in `WorkspaceViewModel` is dropped on tab close and moved on rename.
- Measured on a desktop JVM: a 256 KB buffer with 5,000 decorations costs about 1 ms per keystroke to diff and shift. A paint-window query costs 5 us.
